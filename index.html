Coverage

One extension, every entry point.

Phishing doesn't only arrive as a suspicious link — it hides in look-alike domains, urgent login forms, QR codes on posters, and files disguised as invoices. ThreatLens watches all of it.

Real-time site analysis

Domain age, redirect chains, obfuscated URLs and reputation signals are checked the instant a page loads — before you type a password.

Brand impersonation detection

Catches look-alike domains and typosquats of banks, payment providers and government portals, and names the brand it's mimicking.

Page & form behavior

Reads the DOM for password fields, OTP prompts and urgent language — the same tells a trained analyst looks for, applied to every page.

QR-code inspection

Decodes any QR code rendered on a page and checks its destination before you ever scan it with your phone.

Download protection

Flags double extensions, mismatched file types and risky archive contents, with hash-based reputation lookups.

Webmail link scanning

Highlights dangerous links directly inside Gmail and Outlook web, before you click through from an email.

Under the hood

From page load to protection, in milliseconds.

Every check runs through the same pipeline — a gradient-boosted model trained on phishing patterns, scored and explained before you ever see a warning.

01

Capture

The extension reads the URL, DOM structure and any links, QR codes or downloads on the page.

02

Analyze

An XGBoost model scores dozens of features against threat intelligence and domain reputation data.

03

Explain

SHAP attribution turns the model's output into the plain-language reasons you actually see.

04

Act

Depending on the score, ThreatLens allows, warns, or blocks the page — you can always continue anyway.
